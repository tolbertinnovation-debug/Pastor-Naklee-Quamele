About the Pastor

A heart for people, a calling to serve.

Pastor Naklee Quamele has dedicated his life to walking alongside others through every season — joy and grief, doubt and breakthrough. His teaching is rooted in scripture, delivered with warmth, and aimed at one thing: helping people encounter a love that transforms.

Whether from the pulpit or across a kitchen table, Pastor Naklee believes the church is not a building but a family — one that shows up, listens deeply, and carries hope into the everyday.

  • ● Biblical, grace-centered teaching
  • ● Pastoral care & counseling
  • ● Outreach & community service
